How this works, and its limits
  • Every morning a GitHub Action reads the official recruitment pages of central universities, IITs, NITs, IISERs, AIIMS, state universities and every state’s Public Service Commission or college service commission, and lists links that look like faculty recruitment notices. Sites that block servers outside India are read by a second check that runs on a computer in India whenever it is switched on.
  • Notices for people who already applied — interview calls, postponements, rank lists, scores — are hidden unless you tick “Also show updates for applicants”.
  • Each new notice is opened and read (scanned PDFs are read with OCR) to find its subjects, posts, type of appointment and last date. This is automatic and can miss or mis-read things: always open the official notice before applying.
  • Government colleges in most states recruit through their state commission, so they appear under that commission. Private and aided colleges are not covered.
  • Cards marked “Verified details” were checked by hand on 20 Nov 2026 (Haryana and Punjab).
  • Usual eligibility for Assistant Professor (UGC Regulations 2018): a Master’s degree with 55% marks (5% relaxation for reserved categories) plus NET/SET, or a Ph.D. Many states also need the state language up to Class 10.
  • “My status” marks are saved only in this browser on this device.
