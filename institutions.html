Government colleges in most states hire regular Assistant Professors through the state commission (HPSC, PPSC, RPSC, UPPSC/UPESSC) or the higher education department, so those pages are checked too; colleges themselves post extension lecturer, guest faculty and (in aided colleges) regular posts. Haryana's list comes from the Directorate of Higher Education's college directory; Punjab's from the Department of Higher Education. To add or fix an institution, edit sources.json.
